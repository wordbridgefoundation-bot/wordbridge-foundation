PARTNERSHIPS & OUTREACH

Stronger together, through education.

We believe that expanding access to education requires collaboration. By connecting with schools, libraries, community organizations, and educational initiatives, we hope to bring meaningful learning opportunities to more children.

OUR APPROACH

Connecting people through a shared purpose.

The Wordbridge Foundation welcomes opportunities to connect with organizations and individuals who share an interest in literacy, multilingual education, and accessible learning resources. Through collaboration, we aim to support communities and help educational materials reach the children who can benefit from them.
