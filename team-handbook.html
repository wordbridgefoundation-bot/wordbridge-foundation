Our Team Handbook

A guide to membership, responsibilities, communication, and the standards that shape our work at Wordbridge Foundation.

01 · WELCOME

Welcome to Wordbridge Foundation.

Wordbridge Foundation is a youth-led educational initiative dedicated to making literacy, reading, and learning resources more accessible to children.

Our work is guided by the belief that education should be accessible across languages, cultures, and communities.

Our shared purpose: Every contribution should help advance our educational mission and support meaningful learning opportunities.

02 · RECRUITMENT

Recruitment & Applications

Recruitment is intended to bring together individuals who are interested in education, literacy, research, and community engagement.

Application process

  1. Interested individuals complete the official application form.
  2. Applications are reviewed by the appropriate organizational leadership.
  3. Applicants are evaluated according to their interests, experience, availability, and suitability for the role.
  4. Selected applicants receive an official response.
  5. Accepted members receive onboarding information.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance or a particular position.

03 · SELECTION

Applicant Evaluation

Applicants should be considered fairly and consistently. Selection should be based on relevant qualifications and demonstrated interest, rather than popularity or personal relationships.

Evaluation considerations

  • Interest in the foundation's mission.
  • Relevant skills and experience.
  • Writing and communication abilities where relevant.
  • Reliability and availability.
  • Willingness to learn and collaborate.
  • Suitability for the responsibilities of the position.

Applicant information should remain confidential and only be accessible to authorized reviewers.

04 · ONBOARDING

Membership & Onboarding

New members should receive a clear introduction to the foundation, their responsibilities, and the projects they may participate in.

New member orientation

  • Introduction to Wordbridge Foundation's mission and vision.
  • Explanation of the member's assigned role.
  • Overview of relevant projects and activities.
  • Introduction to communication procedures.
  • Review of organizational expectations.
Members should have an opportunity to ask questions before beginning assigned responsibilities.

05 · ORGANIZATION

Roles & Responsibilities

Founder & Executive Director

  • Establish organizational direction and priorities.
  • Oversee projects and organizational activities.
  • Coordinate recruitment and membership decisions.
  • Review major publications and public communications.

Research Associates

  • Conduct research related to assigned projects.
  • Prepare written reports and research summaries.
  • Support educational resource development.
  • Use reliable sources and acknowledge references.

Project Contributors

  • Complete assigned project tasks.
  • Collaborate with other members.
  • Submit work according to agreed deadlines.

Additional departments and leadership positions may be established as the foundation grows.

06 · EXPECTATIONS

Member Expectations

All members are expected to:

  • Understand and respect the foundation's mission.
  • Complete accepted assignments responsibly.
  • Communicate when they need assistance.
  • Respect other members and their contributions.
  • Submit original work or properly credit sources.
  • Respect confidentiality and organizational materials.
  • Maintain appropriate professional conduct.

07 · COMMUNICATION

Communication Guidelines

Clear and respectful communication helps our projects remain organized and productive.

  • Use designated communication channels for organizational matters.
  • Keep messages respectful and relevant.
  • Respond to important organizational messages within a reasonable period.
  • Notify the relevant project lead about questions or concerns.
  • Do not share private member or applicant information without authorization.

08 · ACCOUNTABILITY

Deadlines & Accountability

Deadlines should be communicated clearly and agreed upon before assignments begin.

  • Members should understand the scope of each assignment.
  • Progress updates should be provided when requested.
  • Members should communicate early if they cannot meet a deadline.
  • Extensions may be considered when circumstances require them.
  • Repeated lack of communication may result in a review of assigned responsibilities.
We value accountability, but we also recognize that our members may have school, family, and other personal commitments.

09 · PROFESSIONALISM

Professional Conduct

Members represent the foundation when participating in its activities and communicating on its behalf.

  • Treat all individuals with respect.
  • Avoid discrimination, harassment, and bullying.
  • Do not misrepresent your position or authority.
  • Obtain approval before making official public statements on behalf of the foundation.
  • Protect the privacy and safety of children and other participants.
  • Do not collect or publish children's personal information without appropriate authorization and safeguards.

10 · MEMBERSHIP CHANGES

Membership Changes

Members who wish to step away from the foundation should communicate with organizational leadership.

Responsibilities may be reassigned when necessary to ensure continuity of ongoing projects.

Membership changes should be handled respectfully, with appropriate attention to confidentiality and unfinished assignments.

FINAL NOTE

Growing together.

Wordbridge Foundation is a growing initiative. Our procedures may evolve as our projects, membership, and organizational needs develop.

This handbook serves as an internal guide and may be updated by organizational leadership.
